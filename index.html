Research · PEAK Award

Detecting Partisan Gerrymandering

Independent research, Northeastern University · 2026–present

Can you prove a map is gerrymandered? I built a pipeline in Python with GerryChain that generates hundreds of legally valid congressional maps by Markov chain Monte Carlo, then places the enacted map inside that distribution. Across 500 sampled maps, Texas's 2025 map produced 8 Democratic seats and fell at the 0th percentile; not one neutrally drawn map was that extreme.

TBCA

Northeastern, Data Science research · Leuven, Belgium · 2026

TBCA (The Best Choice Academics) is a team app in Streamlit, Flask, and MySQL that pairs EU university data with a decade of Eurostat labor statistics, so students can find a best-fit university, statisticians can forecast employment, and budget planners can trace funding to outcomes. My part was the labor side: a 2,354-row panel across 27 countries, an ISCED-NACE crosswalk mapping fields to industries, catching a near-perfect R² that was really temporal leakage, and a budget-reallocation model that flagged computer science as underfunded and engineering as overfunded for Belgium.

Robotics · first author

Optimizing Control

NYU Tandon, Machines in Motion Lab · 2024

A comparative study of three control strategies, PID, MPC, and LQR, on the NYU-Finger robot performing a repetitive kicking task in PyBullet. I benchmarked accuracy, energy, and adaptability across all three: MPC held the smoothest and most energy-efficient trajectory, while LQR responded fastest at the cost of higher energy and overshoot. First-author poster.
